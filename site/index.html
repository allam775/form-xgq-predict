03 OPEN DATA POLICY

Transparency is part of the model.

OpenFootball provides openly licensed fixture records but may be delayed. StatsBomb Open Data includes select historical per-shot event files, not live coverage of every league. We will not use unlicensed feeds to silently fill gaps.
